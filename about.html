About Sensei Gammon

Every teacher knows the moment: the lesson has landed early, the bell is five minutes away, and thirty pairs of eyes are looking at you. Sensei Gammon is here for exactly that moment.

It’s a free collection of quick, fun classroom time-fillers — riddles, word scrambles, “would you rather” questions, quizzes and more — designed to go straight onto your interactive whiteboard. No sign-up, no setup, no fuss. Pick one and you’re away.

Who it’s for

  • Teachers, teaching assistants and cover supervisors in UK schools.
  • Pupils aged 8 to 17, with every activity sorted into age bands (8–11, 11–14 and 14–17).
  • Anyone with 2 to 15 spare minutes and a room full of learners.

How it works

  • Use the filters to choose by time, age, group size and energy level — or just hit 🎲 Random filler.
  • Every activity has big, back-of-the-room text and a fullscreen button.
  • Keyboard shortcuts: Space for next, R to reveal, F for fullscreen.

Safe for school

No adverts, no cookies, no tracking and no accounts. Nothing your pupils type or do is collected. See our privacy page for the details.

Free, and staying free

Sensei Gammon is made by one person in their spare time. If it’s saved your lesson, you can buy Sensei a coffee — but there’s no pressure at all. The site is open source on GitHub.
