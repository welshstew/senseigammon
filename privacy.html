Privacy

The short version: we don’t collect anything.

  • No cookies. This site doesn’t set any cookies at all.
  • No tracking or analytics. We don’t count visitors, record clicks or use any analytics tools.
  • No adverts. There are no ads and no advertising networks.
  • No accounts. Nobody — teacher or pupil — needs to sign up or log in.
  • No data collected. Things you type, like names in the name picker or team scores, stay in your browser tab’s memory only. They are never saved or sent anywhere, and they disappear when you close or refresh the page.
  • No outside scripts or fonts. Everything on the site comes from the site itself, so no third parties see your visit.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub’s servers may briefly process technical information such as IP addresses in order to deliver the pages and keep the service secure. We don’t have access to that information and don’t use it. You can read GitHub’s privacy statement for more.

Buy Me a Coffee

The “Buy Sensei a coffee” button is a plain link. Nothing is loaded from Buy Me a Coffee unless you click it, and then you’re on their website and their privacy policy applies.

Children

The site is designed with the UK Age Appropriate Design Code (the “Children’s Code”) in mind: no profiling, no nudges, no data collection and no third-party content.
